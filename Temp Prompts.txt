They are traditionally considered the "Supreme Arts" because they model the universe's macroscopic and microscopic movements by calculating the interactions between Heaven, Earth, and Man.

The three systems that make up the San Shi are:

Primary Focus: Cosmic and macro-level events, state affairs, natural disasters, dynastic changes, and large-scale warfare.
How it works: It focuses on Heavenly Time and tracks the movement of the Tai Yi (Supreme One) celestial body through different palaces. It is considered the most prestigious and tightly guarded of the three, historically restricted to royal astrologers.

2. Qi Men Dun Jia (奇門遁甲) — The Earth Disc
Primary Focus: Geographical positioning, military strategy, calculating optimal timing for physical movement, and competitive advantages.
How it works: It focuses on Earthly Direction. By analyzing the relationship between time and space, it calculates the "hidden" paths (Dun Jia) to help a person be in the right place at the exact right time to achieve victory or escape harm.

3. Da Liu Ren (大六壬) — The Man Disc
Primary Focus: Daily human affairs, personal relationships, business transactions, specific events, and immediate situational outcomes.
How it works: It focuses on Human Affairs. It uses a complex calculation involving the Earthly Branches, Heavenly Stems, and 12 Personified Spirits to create a snapshot of a exact moment in time, mapping out the hidden motives and eventual trajectory of a specific human situation.
